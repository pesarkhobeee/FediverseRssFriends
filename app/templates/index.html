Reading is better together

Six reasons to bring your feeds here
01
Your own radar
Bundle any RSS or Atom feeds into categories. Auto-refreshed in the background, always current.
02
Follow and copy
See a friend's radar, pick out a single feed, or copy the whole thing into yours with one click.
03
Public, unlisted, or private
Share your reading list on a personal page — or keep it entirely to yourself.
04
Bookmark what matters
Save items for later and follow curators whose taste you trust. Wallabag integration included.
05
Everything is RSS
Your reading list, bookmarks, and activity are all subscribable feeds themselves. Turtles all the way down.
06
No tracking, no ads
Magic-link auth, self-hostable, one SQLite file. Your reading habits stay yours.
